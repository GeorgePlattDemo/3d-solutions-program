# Evidence

*Defined Demand, Local Wood, Useful Work · 3D Solutions Program · October 2026*

The Program asks whether better-defined demand can make useful component work worthwhile beside existing yard stock. Four bodies of evidence give that question substance: North Carolina’s wood economy, distributed-manufacturing research, the earlier patented architecture, and the present Project 1 digital specimen.

They support a concrete investigation. Physical capability and service economics require their own measured results.

## North Carolina’s changing wood economy

*Authority category: Historical Evidence*

NC Commerce’s **Not Your Grandfather’s Manufacturing**, published August 31, 2023, reports that furniture-factory employment fell **59.4% between 1993 and 2022**. It discusses globalization, trade deficits, efficiency, and automation in broader manufacturing changes. The figure establishes the scale of employment loss; it does not identify a single cause or a replacement service. \[1\]

NC State Extension’s **Economic Contribution of the Forest Sector in North Carolina, 2024** reports total economic output up **3.1%** and total supported employment down **1.9%** from 2023. It reports approximately **66,508 direct jobs** and **140,919 jobs including indirect and induced effects**. These sector-wide estimates are distinct from factory employment counts. The two percentages alone do not establish why output and employment moved differently. \[2\]

Extension’s September 2020 **The North Carolina Sawmill Industry: A Closer Look** reports a decline from **229 sawmill companies in 2001 to the low 130s by the late 2010s**, and a state producing more lumber than it consumed. Those are historical observations. The review also discusses consolidation and subsequent employment growth, so fewer companies must not be treated as an equivalent decline in output or jobs. \[3\]

In 2024, Weyerhaeuser indefinitely curtailed its New Bern sawmill in Vanceboro, affecting **73 employees**. The company cited market conditions, cost structure, lack of scale, and limited integration. Woodworking Network records that explanation for one facility. It illustrates the importance of operating scale without establishing that only large facilities can succeed. \[4\]

The Program asks what a different scale of work might do beside the wood: one board, one opening, one customer’s result, prepared without reconstructing the job at every handoff. The employment record supplies context. Demand for that particular service still has to be established.

## Technology can change the comparison

*Authority category: Historical Evidence*

For a small, specific job, interpreting the request, preparing it for production, supplying material, and delivering the result may determine whether the work is worthwhile. Distributed-manufacturing research examines that larger path.

Srai and colleagues’ 2016 **Distributed manufacturing: scope, challenges and opportunities** examines how production technologies, digital coordination, and infrastructure can change manufacturing’s organization and location. Published in the *International Journal of Production Research*, **54(23), 6917–6935**, it considers production closer to end-user demand, personalization, and localized resources. \[5\]

Its economic distinction matters here. Local customization can cost more to manufacture than a mass-produced equivalent while offering greater flexibility. For some low-demand products, inventory, transport, and shrinkage change the comparison. That supports comparing the complete service rather than an isolated cutting operation.

Helu, Sobel, Nelaturi, Waddell, and Hibbard’s 2020 **Industry Review of Distributed Production in Discrete Manufacturing**, published in the *Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering*, **142(11), 110802**, examines matching part requirements to process capabilities under technical, business, and logistical constraints. Its manuscript identifies human planning in quoting and repeated engineering and process-development costs as burdens on distributed production. It also emphasizes controlled, verifiably correct processes when small quantities leave little room for scrap. \[6\]

That research supports measuring preparation as part of the complete service. Reusable compilation may remove repeated drawing, coordinate entry and order-specific programming while shifting specialist work into initial registration, validation, change control and maintenance. The trial must count both sides of that comparison, including unsupported requests and interventions.

NC State Industry Extension Services describes process assessment and technologies matched to specific applications. The North Carolina Manufacturing Extension Partnership supports manufacturing productivity and technology adoption. They identify relevant regional support capacity; no participation or endorsement is claimed. \[7, 8\]

## Source-plan language and independent manufacturing meaning

*Authority category: Current System Behavior for the bounded demonstration; Future Work for general plan ingestion*

The governing distinction is between a plan's wording and its measurable requirements. Printed or customer-provided plans may use “bevel” for a face-miter cut, call a pointed drilled depression “spotface,” or require a “pilot hole” without specifying diameter, depth or reference. The plan's original phrase and revision remain attributable evidence. They cannot confer registered machine capability.

System is responsible for physical part geometry, feature identities, dimensions, datums and openly unresolved differences. The adopted interpretation contract requires a missing angled plane/relation/datum to remain unresolved rather than be guessed from a word. The published cut-package translator and typed recipe compiler use the same end-meaning gate. Published feature constraints reach the Store contract or an attributable blocker; this bounded enforcement does not establish unrestricted plan ingestion. Store alone evaluates the requested geometry against its registered tools, material, stock control and time/Q model; where the request is unsupported it returns an attributable refusal. Program examines whether that reliable interpretation and reusable compilation can reduce the preparation burden. Neither the compiler nor this research record claims that unrestricted PDF/CAD ingestion is already installed.

A general plan ingestion layer remains future work: it must identify the source document/page/revision and any dimensional derivation or contradiction, preserve unsupported and abandoned demand, and require human verification when a drawing does not settle physical geometry. A successful plan reading is not a successful Store evaluation, a machine-ready packet, commissioned operation, or an inspected part. See the controlling [System manufacturing terminology definitions](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/new-system/blob/main/docs/DEFINITIONS.md#manufacturing-terminology-source-words-versus-physical-requirements), [Store specification](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/store-zero/blob/main/docs/STORE-ZERO-SPECIFICATION.md) and current Project 1 digital proof; those must describe the same executable revisions.

## Architecture and provenance

*Authority category: Historical Evidence*

The application supplies meaningful controls at Intent and the Bench. System preserves the customer's choices, named derivations, complete component/feature manifest and definition revision. Store evaluates that inquiry afresh and returns its own stock resolution, operation ledger, modeled time, Q and receipt. Accepted-job custody preserves those exact facts for verified lowering; a price or a customer decision alone does not supply local readiness.

Machine functions are registered before the customer arrives. The customer supplies the required operations and their values; System derives job meaning, component geometry and features; Store resolves stock, capability and the manufacturing plan. The compilation chain joins those requirements to registered tooling, station locations, datums and motion rules.

Its purpose is to absorb job-specific programming burden: supported orders reuse identified engineering instead of requiring another person to interpret, redraw and program each component. Program investigates whether the preparation effort saved exceeds the cost of registering, maintaining and operating that capability.

## Current System Behavior — digital integration evidence

*Authority category: Current System Behavior*

The D-001 test function is an identified-location shallow drilled spot, not a general drilling or flat-bottom spotfacing capability. Its Store registration specifies a 3/16-inch drill, 118° included point and 3/16 inch of full-diameter penetration beyond that point (approximately 0.24383 inch total tip travel). This is the configured reference and ceiling. Project 1's two default spots are derived from the customer's selected part length as a center rule; other admitted definitions may state supported coordinates. The adopted Store contract requires positive separately stated full-diameter depths up to the 3/16-inch maximum to govern each feature's point-height-adjusted Z target, modeled travel and time. The maintained Store reference proof includes independent 1/8-inch and 1/16-inch full-diameter depths at part-relative X=5.25 and 10.25 inches, alongside the unchanged 16/18-inch nominal specimens. Its serialized requests, accepted packets and returned Z movements establish that bounded digital transform; the System browser artifact separately proves the actual application handoff under its identified revisions. Source integration, required checks and deployed identity must be established before describing a reviewed revision as production. Excessive depth, unsupported locations, diameters and physical functions require attributable refusal. The System conformance register owns the integration and deployed-identity distinctions. The unchanged 16/18-inch Project 1 baseline still uses the registered nominal maximum; any selected variation requires a fresh definition, evaluation and current proof. Store must not silently discard an explicitly stated diameter, depth or location during System translation. The [laboratory trial](SUPERVISED-LABORATORY-CELL-TRIAL.md) has the future physical validation obligations.

The current integration proof exercises published derivation, translation, independent manifest, fresh Store evaluation, customer acceptance, exact packet export and bound occupancy replay for two SPF definitions:

## Bounded composition evidence — additional component work

*Authority category: Current candidate implementation and local browser evidence; integration and deployment remain separately recorded*

The registered component tool extends structured definitions without adding another library tile or manufacturing evaluator. Its first independent job defines two nominal 1×6 shelf blanks, two nominal 1×6 side blanks and two nominal 1×2 rails. Starting lengths are 24 inches for shelves/rails and 8 inches for sides, all with square parallel end planes. A separate 30-inch shelf/rail revision tests changed demand. These are component blanks; assembly, fastening, load capacity and physical suitability are not established by their digital supportability.

The same registered `part-groups` compiler rule produces identified components, source inputs, derivation trace and a content revision. Shared admission, transport, the published System adapter and independent Store produce complete coverage and fresh receipts. Local Chromium acceptance uses the real public tool controls, the existing simulated customer decision and packet export, and Store verification/lowering/replay. It retains invalid-input blocking, overlong-part and repeated-inquiry refusal, and rejection of an altered accepted packet. Physical admission remains blocked with zero commands. The System [extension contract](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/new-system/blob/main/SYSTEM-MASTER.md#88-new-projects-and-capability-extensions) and [conformance register](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/new-system/blob/main/SYSTEM-MASTER.md#155-manufacturing-contract-conformance-register) identify the candidate and required release evidence. The generated browser proof includes the additional job with the current Project 1 evidence; local success alone does not establish a public deployment.

This establishes bounded digital composition under the same operations. It does not establish universal plan interpretation, additional physical capability or a commissioned service. The laboratory must separately test whether those component classes can be produced, inspected and handled at worthwhile complete cost.

## Future Work — capability impact and extensibility

*Authority category: Future Work*

A capability-impact engine is a core successor requirement. Preserve complete requested work and each refusal/unresolved capability gap; evaluate separately registered hypothetical tools, stations, ranges or machines against that same demand through deterministic compilation and Store evaluation. Compare newly feasible work, modeled time/Q and full investment, maintenance, labor and utilization costs. Proposed feasibility does not establish a current offering or physical release. Learning can suggest patterns; deterministic requirements and registered capabilities decide satisfaction.

Adding a third or fourth machine must extend the registered capability/execution model without rebuilding the customer-definition compiler. This is an architectural acceptance test. No current prototype or planning narrative is treated as an installed capability-impact service.

## Future Work — evidence needed next

*Authority category: Future Work*

Would you use the [current application](https://system-candidate-production.up.railway.app/) to define a replacement wood component or explore a supported finished object? Try a case and tell us what you needed, what worked, and whether you would use the service. Your response is evidence about demand.

| **Activity**                    | **What it must establish**                                                                                     |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Demand and baseline observation | Actual requests, customer decisions, existing alternatives, and preparation burden.                            |
| Engineering validation          | Stock control, references, tooling, controller execution, and inspected specimens under authorized conditions. |
| Bounded service pilot           | Customer results and whole-job economics using an established capability.                                      |

Hold the required customer result constant when comparing alternatives. Measure clarification, preparation, loading, processing, inspection, rework, staging and handoff. Record per-order programming and re-entry separately from one-time function registration, controller integration and subsequent calibration or tooling changes. Count material, tooling, maintenance, software, installation, space, downtime, capital burden and realistic utilization.

Distinguish improved service from additional demand. A customer-reported abandoned project is useful evidence, but it does not prove what would have happened without the service. Interest is not a purchase, and local production alone does not establish additional employment or an environmental benefit.

The next question is concrete: does carrying a defined request to a bounded machine beside yard stock remove enough burden, and deliver enough customer value, to make the complete service worthwhile?
