# Recovered Grok machine research

Candidate Program research selection, September 24, 2026. Acceptance here means preserving useful questions for review. It does not establish an implemented design or expand the Store envelope. This supplements [the bounded-cell trial](bounded-cell-trial.md); it does not replace it.

The selected ideas below come from Grok's branch-only Atlas research. Each selected source already has an identical public System copy at the pinned paths below. This is a short synthesis, not republication of the private workshop or a new software license. Vendor, market and performance assertions in the historical sources have not been revalidated and are not adopted here.

## 1. Neutral operations and machine translation

Which neutral part operations, datums and reference checks are sufficient to translate a defined job into a particular cell's operations without putting machine commands in the application? Treat POSITION_VALID as a research requirement to define and demonstrate, not evidence that positioning is already verified.

## Adoption boundary

**Proposed design → experimental evidence → reviewed decision → versioned Store/System adoption.**

A future experiment needs an identified configuration, measured outcomes, limitations and an owner review. A positive result still needs a separate versioned Store change and compatible System verification before the application can rely on it. This pass supplies none of those later approvals. Missing geometry, cycle time, commissioning evidence or safety validation remains missing.

The recovered cell-spine variant's claim of live capability was explicitly corrected to synthetic, not installed or commissioned, in its successor. Its recovered definitions remain referenced in System at their existing scope. They are not silently promoted into an admitted machine configuration here. See the [reconciliation report](../migration/grok-reconciliation.md) and [complete source register](../migration/grok-reconciliation.json).
