## Research question

What changes if **incomplete demand becomes usable information earlier**, before a person or business has reduced the need to a SKU, RFQ, conventional order, or specialist vocabulary?

The proposition is not that every incomplete want should become a project.

It is that the interval between an ordinary-language need and a conventional commercial order may contain useful, attributable information that can call existing evidence and bounded productive capability — or receive an honest refusal — without prematurely committing physical material or commercial identity.

## Five information relationships

The source research distinguishes five related but non-equivalent information relationships:

1. **Interest** — what a person voluntarily cares about.
2. **Intent** — what someone is trying to make true in a particular situation.
3. **Evidence** — what is known, by whom, from what source, with what limit.
4. **Capability** — what an identified holder can do, under which prerequisites/envelope/refusal.
5. **Outcome** — what actually happened and what the holder elects to preserve.

They are not one graph.

Interest can exist without project intent.  
Intent can exist without nearby capability.  
Evidence can exist without being callable at the moment of need.  
Capability can exist without being described in language a stranger can use.  
Outcome can remain private and never become general evidence.

Connecting those records does not imply common ownership of them.

## Information before physical commitment

The research treats informational and physical commitment as separable.

A need may become clearer before stock is cut, moved, reserved, or transformed.

That is the Program meaning behind **data before atoms / information before atoms**:

- resolve what can be resolved in information first;
- keep physical material generic where useful;
- commit form only when the identified requirement and applicable capability justify doing so.

This is a research proposition and architecture principle, not a claim that every project should be postponed or locally fabricated.

## Minimum useful objects

The source paper proposes several small information objects as a way to test the proposition.

### Declared need / intent

Preserve what the person actually says without silently turning inference into declaration or a commercial profile.

The record may be incomplete.

### Evidence with a visible limit

Useful evidence must preserve:

- source/holder;
- what was actually studied or established;
- applicable place/time/population/conditions;
- what was not measured;
- date/version.

A smoother summary may not erase the limit.

### Callable capability including refusal

A useful capability description should say what the holder actually offers, accepted materials/forms, hard bounds, prerequisites, and what it refuses.

A readable **no** is part of capability, not a failed conversion.

### A first act useful without a sale

The earliest useful interaction may simply help someone clarify a need, save a bounded declaration, compare legitimate paths, find applicable evidence/capability, or receive a grounded refusal.

Lead capture is not the definition of success.

## Model boundary

A language model, if used, is an interpreter/router across attributed information.

It may help:

- clarify an incomplete sentence;
- retrieve and compare attributed evidence;
- explain a holder-declared capability;
- expose legitimate options and unresolved conditions.

It is not:

- the database;
- the source of institutional authority;
- the owner of private records;
- the Store;
- the machine controller;
- a physical authorization issuer.

As information approaches physical execution, the architecture must become more deterministic, attributable, validated, and authority-bounded.

## Refusal and quality of resolution

A correct refusal is a useful outcome.

The system should not optimize every interaction toward one transaction.

Possible legitimate resolutions include:

- buy an existing product;
- repair;
- modify;
- make locally;
- use an established manufacturer;
- refer to a qualified actor;
- defer;
- refuse;
- abandon;
- learn and retain the record.

A repeated refusal may become a research question. It does not automatically justify a new capability.

## Custody

The research does not require one central database of customer intent, firm capability, institutional knowledge, prices, or proprietary telemetry.

Private project context remains holder-controlled unless shared under an applicable rule.

Firm capability remains with the firm; only the bounded information it elects to publish becomes callable.

Public evidence remains attributable to its source.

## Research sequence that can stop

The source proposes a falsifiable sequence rather than an assumed build program:

```
Place
  ↓
Origination
  ↓
Contribution
  ↓
Trust
  ↓
Interface
  ↓
Execution
```

Each study can narrow, defer, or stop the next.

### 1. Place
Is there a bounded geography/job class where a practical local middle is worth examining?

### 2. Origination
Can incomplete needs be declared ethically in a form that is actually useful without covert lead capture?

### 3. Contribution
Will evidence/capability holders contribute or permit bounded callable information?

### 4. Trust
Are attribution, consent/custody, and refusal rules understandable and credible?

### 5. Interface
Can an ordinary person use those objects without already knowing specialist vocabulary?

### 6. Execution
Only if earlier evidence supports it: can a resolved need be fulfilled or refused by a real operation under a written and demonstrated envelope?

The program must be able to produce **stop** or **nowhere** as legitimate findings.
