## Current organization status

Organization and donor-mining work is in progress as of September 24, 2026. Program owns research, experiments, evidence, machine-development questions/findings, reviewed decisions/adoption records, partnerships/economic/business work, and migration/retirement records. System owns the application and canonical shared operational definitions. Store owns its local material/capability/economic facts and answers. Retired donors are not archived until their pointer/dependency gates are closed.

The bounded-cell document has a reviewed destination here. Review's existing copy and navigation remain intact until a separate relocation change is checked and adopted. Its publication does not establish physical commissioning or a completed Store capability.

## Keep the active record useful

Accepted work, explicit questions, results and decisions belong in the active record. Duplicate copies, obsolete implementation variants and working conversations do not become current merely because they can be recovered. Keep a concise disposition and source identity when an item is excluded; do not copy the entire rejected item into another active folder.

Run `python3 tools/check_program.py` to check this repository's local links, migration evidence and source identities. This is a documentary consistency check, not an application or machine-capability test.
