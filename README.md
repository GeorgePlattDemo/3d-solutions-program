# 3D Solutions Program

**Information travels anywhere. Wood doesn't. What happens when you send one to the other?**

## The question

This Program investigates a practical middle between one-off manual yard work and distant custom-shop or factory processing:

> **Can better-defined demand, sent to wood already sitting in a yard, make a small amount of local processing worth doing — without turning the yard into a factory?**

That question is not answered in advance.

A useful result may be yes, no, only for certain project classes, only under certain economics, or not with the machine assumptions currently being considered. The Program exists to make those distinctions visible before a research idea is mistaken for an operating capability.

## What exists now

Three public repositories carry different parts of the work:

| Owner | What it owns | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Program** | Research questions, evidence, experiments, candidate engineering, adoption records, partnerships/economic work, provenance | That a hypothesis is true merely because it is documented |
| [**System**](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/scan-to-build-system) | The public application and shared operational meaning of an identified job | Live commerce or physical fabrication |
| [**Store**](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/scan-to-build-store) | Store-local facts, declared stock/capability, modeled work/economics, Store answers and refusals | Commissioned machinery or a real-yard business result |

That split is deliberate. Program investigates and records **why**. System defines **what the job means**. Store determines **what this Store can provide**.

The working software demonstrates parts of the information path. The Store provides a declared reference fixture and implemented evaluators. Neither one, by itself or together, establishes market demand, workforce benefit, physical commissioning, or a viable business model.

## The hypothesis being tested

The foundational proposition is simple:

> **Incomplete demand may become useful information earlier than a conventional SKU, RFQ, quote, or order.**

If that proposition holds in a useful bounded case, a person may be able to carry intent and evidence farther before somebody has to redraw, reinterpret, or manually translate the job. A Store may then answer from its own material, capability, and economics — including an honest refusal — without surrendering its own authority.

That is the Program meaning behind **information before atoms**: resolve what can be resolved in information before committing material unnecessarily.

It is a research proposition, not a conclusion. See [Demand as Architecture](research/demand-as-architecture.md).

## What would change the answer

The Program should become more specific only when evidence justifies it.

Useful evidence includes:

- demand that persists after the project is stated clearly;
- refusals that show where capability is actually missing;
- measured physical cycle and handling data;
- material yield and remnant behavior;
- real Store labor and operating constraints;
- actual economics rather than reference estimates;
- operator and workforce observations;
- evidence that a simpler existing path is better.

A repeated refusal may justify another research question. It does not automatically justify another machine feature.

## Participation: what each party can test

This is not a request to accept the architecture. It is an invitation to test the parts that belong to your domain.

- **Independent yards** — Which questions can a yard answer usefully from its existing stock, systems, people, and equipment? Where does the model misunderstand the counter, yard, or fulfillment work?
- **Forestry, extension, and wood-products researchers** — Where is local material actually available, under what conditions, and which value-chain claims have evidence behind them?
- **Machine builders and controls people** — Which candidate mechanisms are practical, which assumptions are weak, and what must be measured before anything could be called commissioned?
- **Workforce and community colleges** — What work remains human, what work changes, what skills would be required, and what should not be automated?
- **Small-business and economic-development researchers** — Under what demand, labor, utilization, and capital conditions could a bounded local processing service make sense — or fail to make sense?
- **Builders and project holders** — Which handoffs actually lose meaning today, and where would a more explicit project definition help or simply add burden?

A negative finding is useful if it is attributable and bounded.

## Limits

The current work includes a public software demonstration, a fictional reference Store, modeled machine capability/economics, research records, and candidate engineering. No physical D-001 or S-001 production cell has been commissioned by this work. The Program has not established that local demand, business economics, workforce effects, or community benefit will support the larger hypothesis.

**Information before atoms. NO BLOOD ON WOOD.**
