# Information custody, disclosure, and external processing

**Status:** Program governance.  
**Owner:** 3D Solutions Program.  
**Source lineage:** reconciles the still-valid substance of Governed Reference information-custody, disclosure-traversal, external-processing, participant-contribution, planned-custody, and aggregate-stewardship notes. Several exact source notes are already public in System as historical governance copies.  
**Does not:** create an implemented consent system, choose a model provider, answer legal compliance questions, or authorize data reuse.

## Core rule

**Connecting useful information does not by itself transfer ownership, custody, authority, or unrestricted reuse rights.**

A record may remain with its holder while a bounded assertion, permission, request, or response is made usable elsewhere.

Immediate project use does not automatically permit:

- research;
- model improvement or training;
- aggregation;
- marketing;
- unrelated commercial profiling;
- publication;
- retention beyond the applicable purpose.

## Declared information stays distinct from inference

A person may declare a need, preference, measurement, source, or constraint.

A system or model may help interpret, classify, compare, or explain it.

Inference must remain attributable as inference. It may not silently become:

- the person's declaration;
- a verified observation;
- a commercial profile;
- a permission;
- an authorization;
- a physical fact.

## Minimum necessary disclosure

Before information is intentionally sent outside the holder-controlled boundary, the applicable workflow should be able to state:

- the present purpose;
- the minimum information necessary for that purpose;
- the intended recipient/processor;
- the source/holder of the information;
- the permission or authority basis;
- known retention/reuse treatment;
- unresolved terms.

A larger payload is not automatically a better payload.

## External processing

A language model or other external processor is a **processor**, not a source of governed or physical authority.

If an external processor is used, preserve the distinction among:

- source evidence;
- disclosed input;
- model/processor output;
- derived result;
- observation;
- recommendation;
- Store answer;
- authorization.

No model call belongs on a non-waivable safety gate or physical authorization path.

Unknown provider, retention, logging, subprocessor, training/improvement, backup, abuse-monitoring, or tool-call terms remain unresolved where those facts materially affect the intended use. A marketing phrase such as “zero data retention” is not, by itself, a complete recorded processing contract.

## Secondary use

A project record is not automatically a research or training dataset.

Secondary use requires a separately stated purpose and an applicable permission/custody rule. De-identification alone does not create unlimited permission.

A later secondary-use mechanism, if built, should keep project-use authority separate from research/training/aggregation authority.

## Aggregation

Private refusals, outcomes, measurements, prices, or project records are not automatically collected into an aggregate.

If a voluntary aggregate is ever proposed, unresolved questions must be explicitly decided, including:

- source-record custody;
- permission to contribute;
- operator/steward;
- permitted use;
- publication authority;
- retention/withdrawal;
- suppression/minimum-group rules where relevant;
- commercial/research use;
- rights in derived tools or recommendations;
- who, if anyone, may approve a resulting system change.

Do not assume 3D Solutions, a university, a state agency, a nonprofit, or contributors collectively own the aggregate merely because they participate.

Frequency is not representative demand without an appropriate sampling basis. Repeated refusal may justify a research question; it does not automatically prove statewide demand or authorize a capability expansion.

## Participant contribution boundary

A participant should be asked only for the smallest useful contribution needed for the stated purpose.

Examples of bounded contributions may include:

- owner: declared need, project evidence, permission for the specific project;
- yard/shop: published operations, material limits, envelope, prerequisites, refusal boundary;
- contractor: bounded install/site constraints relevant to the project;
- college/training shop: training/equipment boundaries relevant to the study;
- manufacturer/source: attributed material/product limits it chooses to publish;
- 3D Solutions: assumptions, interfaces, refusal logic, simulation policy, experiment results.

The architecture does not require customer lists, confidential bids, private margins, workforce rosters, proprietary process recipes, production schedules, or unrelated household information merely to make a bounded capability callable.

## Competition and commercial sensitivity

A shared information layer must not be designed as a venue for coordinating competitors' prices, customer allocation, future pricing, territories, proprietary margins, or competitively sensitive production information.

This is a design boundary, not a legal conclusion. Any live multi-firm network needs appropriate legal review for its actual data and operation.

## Improvement authority

Outcome and refusal evidence may identify a **candidate question**.

It may not automatically alter:

- a rule;
- prompt;
- schema;
- project definition;
- Store envelope;
- recommendation;
- machine capability;
- safety gate.

The chain remains:

```
observation / outcome
        ↓
candidate question
        ↓
bounded study or experiment
        ↓
reviewed evidence
        ↓
recorded decision
        ↓
versioned owner implementation
```

Negative findings remain evidence. A correct safety or authority refusal is not an optimization defect.

## Current implementation consequence

Program owns this governance.

System and Store should implement only the custody/disclosure behavior actually required by their current contracts. They should not retain duplicate governance documents merely to restate this page once Program authority is adopted.

Any future executable consent, disclosure, external-processing, retention, or aggregation object must be separately designed, versioned, tested, and accepted by its implementation owner.
