## Program question

What information-sharing, secondary-use, research, aggregation, participant, and commercial-policy rules would be required if Scan-to-Build grows beyond the current holder-controlled project/record path?

The current System application owns its implemented evidence/record custody and any future operational disclosure interface. Program records the broader questions, evidence, alternatives, and reviewed decisions that may later justify an owner-specific change.

## Research-use boundary

A project record is not automatically a research, model-training, aggregation, marketing, publication, or unrelated profiling dataset.

Immediate project use does not automatically permit a separate secondary use.

Questions that require an explicit Program decision before any owner implementation include:

- research or model-improvement purpose;
- aggregation;
- publication;
- retention beyond the project purpose;
- marketing or unrelated commercial profiling;
- permission withdrawal/expiry;
- rights in derived tools or recommendations.

De-identification alone does not establish unrestricted reuse permission.

## External processor questions

System owns any future operational disclosure/processor interface. Program retains the broader research/policy questions that must be answered before such a feature is responsibly adopted.

For a proposed external processor, unresolved questions may include:

- provider and model/service identity;
- retention and deletion treatment;
- logging;
- subprocessors;
- training/improvement use;
- backup and abuse-monitoring treatment;
- tool/plugin forwarding;
- geographic/organizational custody expectations;
- contractual or policy changes over time.

A marketing phrase such as “zero data retention” is not, by itself, a complete recorded processing arrangement.

A processor remains a processor. Program research must not promote a processor into project, Store, safety, machine, or physical authority.

## Voluntary aggregation

Private refusals, outcomes, measurements, prices, or project records are not automatically collected into an aggregate.

If a voluntary aggregate is proposed, unresolved questions must be explicitly decided, including:

- source-record custody;
- permission to contribute;
- operator/steward;
- permitted use;
- publication authority;
- retention/withdrawal;
- suppression/minimum-group rules where relevant;
- commercial/research use;
- rights in derived tools or recommendations;
- who, if anyone, may approve a resulting System, Store, or machine-development change.

Do not assume 3D Solutions, a university, a state agency, a nonprofit, or contributors collectively own an aggregate merely because they participate.

Frequency is not representative demand without an appropriate sampling basis. Repeated refusal may justify a research question; it does not automatically prove statewide demand or authorize capability expansion.

## Participant contribution boundary

A participant should be asked only for the smallest useful contribution needed for the stated purpose.

Examples of bounded contributions may include:

- owner: declared need, project evidence, and permission applicable to the specific project or study;
- yard/shop: published operations, material limits, envelope, prerequisites, refusal boundary;
- contractor: bounded install/site constraints relevant to the project;
- college/training shop: training/equipment boundaries relevant to the study;
- manufacturer/source: attributed material/product limits it elects to publish;
- 3D Solutions: assumptions, interfaces, refusal logic, simulation policy, and experiment results.

The research architecture does not require customer lists, confidential bids, private margins, workforce rosters, proprietary process recipes, production schedules, or unrelated household information merely to make a bounded capability callable.

## Competition and commercial sensitivity

A shared information layer must not be designed as a venue for coordinating competitors' prices, customer allocation, future pricing, territories, proprietary margins, or competitively sensitive production information.

This is a Program design/research boundary, not a legal conclusion. A live multi-firm network requires appropriate legal review for its actual data and operation.

## Improvement authority

Outcome and refusal evidence may identify a **candidate question**.

It may not automatically alter:

- a rule;
- prompt;
- schema;
- project definition;
- Store envelope;
- recommendation;
- machine capability;
- safety gate.

The adoption chain remains:

```
observation / outcome
        ↓
candidate question
        ↓
bounded study or experiment
        ↓
reviewed evidence
        ↓
recorded decision
        ↓
versioned owner implementation
```

Negative findings remain evidence. A correct safety or authority refusal is not an optimization defect.

## Relationship to current System custody

Current application custody behavior belongs to System, including its actual evidence/source persistence, record history, owner archive, import/reopen semantics, and any later operational disclosure interface.

Program should not duplicate those implementation rules here.

Program may record:

- why a new custody/disclosure capability is being considered;
- evidence and alternatives;
- unresolved legal/provider/stewardship questions;
- research/secondary-use proposals;
- reviewed adoption or rejection decisions.

Any adopted operational change must be implemented and tested by the owning repository.
