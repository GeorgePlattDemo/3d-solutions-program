# Authority

**Program investigates and records why. System defines what the job means. Store determines what the identified Store can provide.**

A document may discuss another owner’s work. It does not gain authority over that work by doing so.

## Responsibilities

| **Owner**                    | **Owns**                                                                                                                                                              | **Boundary**                                                                                              |
|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Program                      | Research questions, evidence, experiments, candidate engineering, machine development, economic and operating research, findings, and reviewed development decisions. | Proposals and findings do not change System behavior, Store capability, or physical operating permission. |
| System                       | The application, project definitions, shared operational meaning, job records and revisions, custody, and interfaces carrying the identified job to Store.            | It preserves and presents Store answers without creating substitute facts, prices, or capability.         |
| Store                        | Its material, catalog, represented stock, admitted capability, modeled work/time, economics, answers, refusals, and Store-specific fulfillment meaning.               | It answers the definition without changing the requested component to make the job fit.                   |
| Local machine implementation | Commissioned geometry, stations, tools, references, lowering/compiler, controller configuration, I/O, motion, interlocks, calibration, and observed physical results. | It adds execution facts without reinterpreting customer requirements or inventing Store authority.        |

Shared operational terms are defined once in [System’s definitions](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/new-system/blob/main/docs/DEFINITIONS.md). Program may investigate or propose a change; operational adoption requires an identified change in the responsible owner.

## Definition and answer

System keeps parts, dimensions, features, operations, and revisions attached to the same job. A changed definition requires a fresh Store evaluation for the revised definition and applicable inquiry scope.

Store can answer yes, no, unresolved, or unavailable according to its declared facts. It needs enough knowledge of a machine to declare a bounded capability; it does not need to contain the machine implementation. A Store answer does not establish physical readiness or permission.

## Machine development and adoption

Candidate machine development belongs in Program while the engineering is investigated. Component selection, sensing, controls, lowering, operating methods, and experiments remain development work until evidence supports adoption.

For an established configuration, Program retains the development record and decision; Store declares the capability it will offer; the local implementation owns commissioned configuration and physical operation. System continues to supply job meaning.

The intended chain is:

**Customer requirement → System definition → Store answer → bounded machine-neutral work → local lowering → controller and machine → observed result.**

No later layer may backfill authority missing from an earlier one.

## Project 1 custody

Store holds the published [Project 1 reference specimen and evidence package](https://github.com/GeorgePlattDemo/store-zero/blob/main/docs/project-1-digital-trail/README.md). That documentary custody does not adopt its reference controller, unresolved mechanical assumptions, or proposed machine as Store capability.

Program retains broader machine development, controller/compiler development, physical experimentation, and adoption work. System retains operational job meaning. A later technical result receives a new identified revision; it does not rewrite the historical specimen.

## One owner and references elsewhere

Put authority where it belongs and reference it elsewhere. Research, a Store declaration, local execution, and inspection are connected acts with distinct owners and evidence.

Research use or publication of customer and business records requires permission for that purpose. Project participation does not itself authorize unrelated reuse. Historical source attribution and applicable notices remain attached to the material retained or referenced.
