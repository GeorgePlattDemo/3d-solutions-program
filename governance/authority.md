## One owner, references elsewhere

A research proposal is a Program fact. An adopted evaluation limit is a Store fact. A physical commissioning result is a machine-engineering evidence record. The application consumes the relevant identified answer without creating one of its own.

Local controller configuration and registered postprocessor engineering belong to the machine-development program. Store declares the specific machine configuration on which its model relies. This register does not move controller code into the application or authorize physical operation.

Shared interface changes require compatible producer and consumer changes. Project identity and project-specific facts remain isolated even where the evaluator is shared.

For shared **operational** terminology, System is the semantic owner. Store may define narrower Store-local terms but does not change the identified job meaning. Program may define research/program vocabulary and record proposals, evidence, and adoption decisions; a Program document does not silently rewrite System or Store behavior. Existing executable behavior remains behavior until deliberately changed and tested in its owning repository.

## Public posture

**Program is the public home for research, experimental development, evidence, reviewed decisions/adoption records, partnership/economic/business work, and migration/retirement records. System is the public application and canonical operational-definition home. Store is the public Store-fact/capability/economics owner. Private donor repositories remain private historical sources unless a specific source body is intentionally approved for publication. Do not bulk-publish donor contents merely because they are being retired.**

Apply that rule as follows:

- publish reconciled operational meaning through System, Store-local facts through Store, and research/evidence/decisions through Program;
- preserve exact private donor identities and SHAs as provenance;
- keep rejected, superseded, failed, misleading, legally unresolved, or merely historical source bodies private unless separately approved;
- a private source may be mined into a public current record without publishing the entire private source;
- credentials, personal records and third-party confidential material do not enter the public Program;
- publication does not itself change copyright, licensing, authorship, patent ownership, technical authority or reuse rights.

A folder name is not an access control. Preserve source attribution and applicable notices.

## Source status

Use separate fields for documentary disposition and implementation evidence. `admitted-document` does not mean `implemented`, `tested`, `deployed` or `commissioned`. Exact content equality establishes duplication, not authority or permission to delete the source.
