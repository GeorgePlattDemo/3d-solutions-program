# Ownership, authorship, and source questions

**Status:** unresolved Program record.  
**Source lineage:** Governed Reference `legal/ownership-questions.md` at main `18949f163718a937f072f4be3a654bb303e53160`.  
**Purpose:** preserve questions that still matter during repository consolidation.  
**Not legal advice.**

Patent ownership, copyright ownership, repository access, and authority to license are separate questions.

The historical source asked questions that remain useful at Program level:

1. Who is the author/copyright owner of the historical STB-REF, STB-PLAN, Demand as Architecture, and Common Entry Contexts works?
2. Were those works created personally, for 3D Solutions LLC, or under another arrangement?
3. Are any written assignments required or already in place?
4. What ownership/assignment rule applies to future contractor contributions?
5. What treatment is intended for model-assisted text/code contributions?
6. What institutional/student policies would apply to university or training-partner contributions?
7. Which third-party material is quoted, summarized, mapped, linked, or incorporated?
8. Who has authority to license or publish each category of Program material?
9. Which repository/service terms materially affect private or public source custody?

## Consolidation rule

Repository cleanup does not answer these questions.

Moving a document for organizational purposes does not by itself:

- transfer copyright;
- grant a license;
- change patent ownership;
- prove authorship;
- waive restrictions;
- authorize public release of a previously private source.

Where a private donor contains substantial unpublished text, Program may record the source identity and admitted propositions without publishing the full source body unless publication is intentionally approved.

## Current patent/source boundary

Issued patents may be cited as primary technical background and maintained where the current project keeps its patent sources.

They do not become runtime rules, machine commissioning evidence, safety certification, or proof that a present implementation practices every disclosed embodiment.

The older Governed Reference preference not to store patent PDFs is a repository-local historical choice, not current Program governance.

## Dependency-license history

The Governed Reference dependency-license table describes the historical M1 package versions only. It is not a current Program dependency inventory.

Current executable repositories remain responsible for their own dependency/license records.

## Review trigger

Resolve or update this record before any action that depends on a legal conclusion about:

- public release of a private source body;
- relicensing;
- third-party contribution ownership;
- university/student contribution terms;
- commercialization or transfer of copyrighted Program materials.

Until then, preserve provenance and avoid turning repository movement into an unstated legal conclusion.
